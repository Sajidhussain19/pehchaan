# for Pehchaan v2. Used by the EVERYDAY-WORD GUARD (app/decide/lexicons.py): a span made ONLY of these words is
# ordinary speech ("ee roju chala vedi" = "today it's very hot"), so it is never turned into a contact, place or

# Family words (Amma, Nanna, Akka, Anna, Bhaiya, Pinni, Thatha...) are deliberately NOT here: they DO refer
# to people (see the reference rules). Spelling variants are matched after squashing doubled letters
# ("chaalaa" == "chala"), so one spelling per word is enough.
